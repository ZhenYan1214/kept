2 · Payment lines

Pseudonymous: an address and an amount

0x3f9c…a1NT$50,000received
0x81d2…7eNT$50,000not received
0xc04b…19NT$50,000no answer

Each household finds its own line and signs with its own key. Lines that are not registered households are counted in public.

3 · Sample audit

For those who never answer

The contract draws a random sample of each batch, seeded by a block fixed at publication. An independent auditor visits; the household signs the visit with its own key, or the auditor records "nobody there" under its own address.

Anyone can compute how much of the batch could be undelivered (95% upper bound).

Who is a household

Registered with two signatures: the village chief and the township office. The credential lives on a phone or a printed card.

4 · The charter

Purposes and caps are fixed when the money is raised. Spending outside them reverts. Changes wait in public, and households can veto them anonymously (Semaphore, one vote per household).

Never on-chain

Names, ID numbers, bank accounts. In phase 1 the money itself stays in banks.
