2Payment lines

Each household finds its own line and signs with its own key. Lines that are not registered households are counted in public.

3Sample audit

For those who never answer

Anyone can make the contract draw a random sample of each batch (1 line in 5), seeded by a block hash just after publication; a later draw uses a later block, recorded publicly. An independent auditor visits; the household signs the visit with its own key, or the auditor records "nobody there" under its own address.

Anyone can compute how much of the batch could be undelivered (95% upper bound).

Who is a household

Registered with two signatures: the village chief and the township office. The credential lives on a phone or a printed card.

4The charter

Purposes and caps are fixed when the money is raised. Spending outside them reverts. Changes wait in public (an emergency move between purposes applies at once and is reviewed afterwards), and households can veto them anonymously (Semaphore, one vote per household).

Never on-chain

Names, ID numbers, bank accounts. In phase 1 the money itself stays in banks.
